About AutoSentinel AI

Industrial Safety Intelligence and Explainable Automotive Defect Analysis.

Executive Platform Summary

AutoSentinel AI is a high-integrity, explainable safety intelligence platform designed for regulators, engineers, and manufacturing executives. By integrating advanced machine learning, deep natural language processing, and robust functional safety protocols, AutoSentinel AI turns raw vehicle recall complaints and campaign logs into actionable, predictive intelligence.

This system ingests, processes, and classifies defect descriptions to forecast severity levels, map high-dimensional vector spaces for semantic similarity, and provide raw, mathematically sound local explanations for every decision via SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations).

AutoSentinel AI Threat Assessment Pipeline Flow

1. Defect Ingest

Raw NLP Defect Prompt + Vehicle Metadata

2. Dual Vectorizing

TF-IDF Vectorizer & all-MiniLM Dense Embedder

3. Safety Overrides

XGBoost Classifiers + ISO 26262 Trigger Overrides

4. Explanations

SHAP marginal traces + BART CNN summary

Technical Model Architectures

1. XGBoost Threat Classifier

Fitted Extreme Gradient Boosting Decision Tree model mapping preprocessed textual features (TF-IDF vectorizations) and numerical dimensions (vehicle age, manufacturer volumes, component categories) to stratified safety risk labels.

2. Explainable AI (SHAP Core)

TreeExplainer engine mapping local marginal Shapley values for exact token-level feature inputs. Reconstructs dense TF-IDF and categorical variables back to natural vocabulary terms, ensuring transparency in every risk verdict.

3. Dense Semantic Vector Engine

SentenceTransformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) embedding encoder translating natural language queries into 384-dimensional dense vectors. Performs real-time cosine-similarity calculations across historical defect campaigns.

4. Hybrid Functional Safety Controls

Refined functional safety trigger layer (conforming to ISO 26262 principles) mitigating data sampling skew. Obvious critical hazards (such as battery thermal runaway, structural airbag ruptures, or complete steering failure) are escalated immediately.
